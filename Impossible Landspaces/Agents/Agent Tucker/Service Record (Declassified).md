# Rex Horne - Declassified Service Record

## 1982 - Honduras/Nicaragua Border

**Location:** 

Puerto Lempira, La Mosquitia – remote jungle outpost on the Río Coco, reachable only by
canoe. Thick humidity, endless mosquitoes, villages half-swallowed by forest.

**Comrades:** 

SSG Thomas 'Tommy' Carver – wiry medic from Kentucky, chain-smoker, kept a Bible
in his pocket. - Lt. Mario Hernández – Honduran officer, proud but superstitious, warned Rex not to
whistle after dark.

**Food & Drink:** 

Pupusas con curtido – hot corn patties stuffed with beans and cheese, vinegar-sharp
cabbage slaw. - Aguardiente – raw sugarcane liquor, metallic burn, soldiers joked it doubled as
antiseptic.

**Occult Encounter:**

Ruined church frescoes showed conquistadors fighting many-armed figures. A
captured prisoner prayed in an unknown tongue before clawing out his eyes.


## 1983 - Grenada (Operation URGENT FURY)

**Location:**
True Blue Campus and Fort Frederick, St. George’s – tropical heat, chaotic firefights.

**Comrades:**

Sgt. Bobby 'Grin' Fallon – Boston native, upbeat, carried a boom box blasting Springsteen.

Capt. Alan Keating – overeager junior officer, nearly lost the squad with bad maps.

**Food & Drink:**

Oil Down – breadfruit, salted fish, coconut stew, turmeric-yellow, rich and hearty. 
Carib Lager – pale, weak beer, but cold enough to feel divine.

**Occult Encounter:**

Evacuated student carried a notebook filled with sketches of vine-wrapped
screaming faces. She disappeared from the evac site without trace.

## 1984 - Colombia

**Location: **

Cali and Putumayo jungle – coca labs hidden in oppressive heat.

**Comrades:**

DEA Agent Victor Morales – gaunt, chain-smoker, cynical, claimed cartels weren’t the
worst predators here. 

Cpl. Jesús 'Chuy' Delgado – cheerful Colombian scout, 20, killed in ambush.

**Food & Drink:** 

Arepas de queso – crisp corn cakes with molten cheese. 

Aguardiente Antioqueño – smoother cane liquor with a licorice tang.

**Occult Encounter:**

Beneath a burned lab lay spiraled human bones. Delgado swore a woman with a candle watched them.

## 1985–1986 – Sudan & Ethiopia

**Location: 

Kassala, Sudan and Asmara, Ethiopia – famine corridors, thorn scrub plains.

**Comrades:

Lt. Col. Ernest 'Ernie' Briggs – older officer, quoted Psalms, steady under pressure. 

Nurse Amara Tesfaye – Ethiopian Red Cross, kind, taught Rex to cook doro wat, gifted him a carved cross.

**Food & Drink: 

Injera – sour, spongy flatbread, tangy and communal. 

Doro Wat – fiery chicken stew with berbere, oily and red. - 

Tej – golden honey wine, deceptively smooth.

**Occult Encounter: 

Dust storm flattened tents as if pressed by a giant hand. Survivors whispered of 'Ash-Eaters.'
