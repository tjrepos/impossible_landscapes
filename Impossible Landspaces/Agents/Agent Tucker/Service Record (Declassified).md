# Rex Horne - Declassified Service Record

## 1982 - Honduras/Nicaragua Border

**Location:** 

Puerto Lempira, La Mosquitia – remote jungle outpost on the Río Coco, reachable only by
canoe. Thick humidity, endless mosquitoes, villages half-swallowed by forest.

**Comrades:** 

SSG Thomas 'Tommy' Carver – wiry medic from Kentucky, chain-smoker, kept a Bible
in his pocket. - Lt. Mario Hernández – Honduran officer, proud but superstitious, warned Rex not to
whistle after dark.

**Food & Drink:** 

Pupusas con curtido – hot corn patties stuffed with beans and cheese, vinegar-sharp
cabbage slaw. - Aguardiente – raw sugarcane liquor, metallic burn, soldiers joked it doubled as
antiseptic.

**Occult Encounter:**

Ruined church frescoes showed conquistadors fighting many-armed figures. A
captured prisoner prayed in an unknown tongue before clawing out his eyes.


## 1983 - Grenada (Operation URGENT FURY)

**Location:**
True Blue Campus and Fort Frederick, St. George’s – tropical heat, chaotic firefights.

**Comrades:**

Sgt. Bobby 'Grin' Fallon – Boston native, upbeat, carried a boom box blasting Springsteen.

Capt. Alan Keating – overeager junior officer, nearly lost the squad with bad maps.

**Food & Drink:**

Oil Down – breadfruit, salted fish, coconut stew, turmeric-yellow, rich and hearty. 
Carib Lager – pale, weak beer, but cold enough to feel divine.

**Occult Encounter:**

Evacuated student carried a notebook filled with sketches of vine-wrapped
screaming faces. She disappeared from the evac site without trace.

## 1984 - Colombia

**Location: **

Cali and Putumayo jungle – coca labs hidden in oppressive heat.

**Comrades:**

DEA Agent Victor Morales – gaunt, chain-smoker, cynical, claimed cartels weren’t the
worst predators here. 

Cpl. Jesús 'Chuy' Delgado – cheerful Colombian scout, 20, killed in ambush.

**Food & Drink:** 

Arepas de queso – crisp corn cakes with molten cheese. 

Aguardiente Antioqueño – smoother cane liquor with a licorice tang.

**Occult Encounter:**

Beneath a burned lab lay spiraled human bones. Delgado swore a woman with a candle watched them.

## 1985–1986 – Sudan & Ethiopia

**Location: 

Kassala, Sudan and Asmara, Ethiopia – famine corridors, thorn scrub plains.

**Comrades:

Lt. Col. Ernest 'Ernie' Briggs – older officer, quoted Psalms, steady under pressure. 

Nurse Amara Tesfaye – Ethiopian Red Cross, kind, taught Rex to cook doro wat, gifted him a carved cross.

**Food & Drink: 

Injera – sour, spongy flatbread, tangy and communal. 

Doro Wat – fiery chicken stew with berbere, oily and red. - 

Tej – golden honey wine, deceptively smooth.

**Occult Encounter: 

Dust storm flattened tents as if pressed by a giant hand. Survivors whispered of 'Ash-Eaters.'


## 1987 – El Salvador

**Location: 

Morazán Province, Torola River villages – jungle hills scarred by war.

**Comrades: 

Sgt. Ray 'Crow' Miller – cynical heavy gunner, vanished during the mission. 

Pvt. Antonio Rivas – Salvadoran scout, devout, fled screaming into the jungle.

**Food & Drink:

Yuca frita – fried cassava root, crisp outside, soft inside. 

Pilsener Lager – cheap, watery beer, staple of local soldiers.

**Occult Encounter: 

Entire village nailed shut, inhuman screams from jungle, melted wax-like corpses.

## 1988 – Honduras (Shadow Op)

**Location: 

La Mosquitia jungle near Gracias a Dios – remote swamps.

**Comrades:

CWO Daniel 'Danny' Cole – pragmatic, declared MIA. 

SSgt. Julian Ortega – sarcastic, Rex’s closest friend, survived but broken.

**Food & Drink: 

Baleadas – tortillas folded with beans, cheese, cream. 

Guaro – raw cane liquor, cloudy and harsh.

**Occult Encounter: 

Safehouse walls bore shifting sigils, tin of human teeth on the table. Teammate vanished, boots left standing upright.

## 1989 – Panama (JUST CAUSE)

**Location: 

Panama City, El Chorrillo, Torrijos International Airport – urban combat.

**Comrades: 

Sgt. Peter 'Dutch' Van Horne – Rex called him 'Cousin,' killed in crossfire. 

Capt. Juan Velasco –Panamanian officer, bitter but professional ally.

**Food & Drink: 

Arroz con pollo – saffron rice, tender chicken, communal dish. 

Seco Herrerano – sharp cane liquor, doubled as paint stripper.

**Occult Encounter: 

Blood sigils freshly painted in a hidden basement, civilians upstairs unaware.

## 1990 – Kuwait (Shadow Op)

**Location: 

Al-Wafra desert, dunes south of Basra – blistering heat.

**Comrades: 

Sgt. Allen 'Brick' Cavanaugh – massive, cheerful, reassigned and disappeared from records. 

PFC Javed al-Khatib –young Kuwaiti fixer, vanished from all reports.

**Food & Drink: 

Machboos – lamb with saffron rice and cardamom. 

Arabic Coffee – bitter, served in tiny ritual cups.

**Occult Encounter: 

Half-buried ziggurat welded shut with U.S. steel plates from 1950s. Chanting echoed from within, though no one was inside.

## 1991 – Gulf War (DESERT STORM)

**Location: 

Saudi-Iraqi border, patrols into southern Iraq – freezing nights, scorching days.

**Comrades: 

Sgt. Maj. Howard 'Howie' Flynn – gruff old-school NCO, skeptical of Rex’s humor. 

Cpl. Nasser Al-Sabah – Kuwaiti liaison, proud, taught Rex Arabic jokes.

**Food & Drink: 

Dates – sticky, sweet, portable energy. 
MRE Chili Mac – despised, Rex joked it violated the Geneva Convention.

**Occult Encounter: 

GPS looped them endlessly to a burned-out tank with American skeletons in old uniforms

## 1992–1994 – Somalia (RESTORE HOPE)

Location: Mogadishu, Baidoa, Kismayo – dusty markets, sniper alleys, UN convoys.
Comrades: - Sgt. 'Red' Daniels – pale sniper, loyal, survived but drank himself into ruin. - Asha Farah –
Somali nurse, devout, taught Rex suqaar, prayed over casualties.
Food & Drink: - Suqaar – smoky beef stir fry with peppers and onions. - Goat Stew – hearty, simple,
best shared. - Somali Gin – contraband, piney, harsh, drunk with nervous laughter.
Occult Encounter: Rex saw a child walk untouched through gunfire before vanishing into an alley.
Locals whispered of djinn walking with the starving.
