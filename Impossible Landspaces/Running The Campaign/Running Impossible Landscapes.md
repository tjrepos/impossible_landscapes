
The Handler must establish the ground rules of surreal horror for the players. Present these  concepts in the first gameplay session so players understand what to expect:

REALITY: Although surreal horrors transcend the everyday world, the Agents’ frame of reference
must begin in their everyday reality. Use mundane references to set the scene so that when madness strikes, it stands out. Agents must believe they are in our world before leaving it becomes of interest.

CONSISTENCY: Agents operate on information presented by the Handler. It is vital that when
the Handler gives information, it remains consistent from minute to minute and from session
to session. When changes occur, they must make sense — even surreal ones, and even if the underlying reasons are beyond comprehension.

UNCERTAINTY: Agents must rarely feel entirely in control. If they do, that only demonstrates how
far out of control the situation truly is.

TENSION: The Handler must balance on the line between revealing horror and hinting at horror.
Without this tension, horrors soon lose their ability to instill fear. Observe your players. Practice
learning to read the table. Learn to sense when a horrific confrontation is needed to keep the paranoia and terror going and when to ease back.
